Part 1 Overview of the Company

1 Corporate History

The Company was established in Osaka Prefecture in March 1953 for the purpose of manufacturing and selling precision components. In 1968, the head office was relocated to Tokyo, and in 1972 the Company was listed on the Second Section of the Tokyo Stock Exchange, and was reassigned to the First Section in 1981.

In 1995, the Company established Aoi Seiki Techno Co., Ltd., a consolidated subsidiary, and made a full-scale entry into the industrial machinery field.

The Group continuously promotes the enhancement of its quality management system and recieves regular external audits.

Part 2 Business Overview

1 Management Policy and Business Environment

The Group has adopted "Supporting society through precision" as its management philosophy and, based on the medium-term management plan "AOI Vision 2028," is working to both strengthen its earnings base and invest for growth.

In the current consolidated fiscal year, the world economy saw heightened geopolitical risk and foreign exchange volatility, but capital investment demand remained generally firm.

Total research and development expenses for the current consolidated fiscal year were 17,400 million yen.

1 Management Policy and Business Environment (continued)

Under the medium-term management plan, the Group targets net sales of 520.0 billion yen and an operating margin of 9.0% for fiscal 2028. To realize management conscious of the cost of capital, the Group is rolling out ROIC-based management across the organization.

The priority issues to be addressed are expanding production capacity, securing engineers, and strengthening the resilience of the supply chain.

The Group is promoting the automation of in-process inspection and is working to detect nonconformities at an early stage.
